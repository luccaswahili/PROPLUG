LookMatch

Match the look of any still, then finish it with film print emulation, halation, bloom and grain.

The installer puts one plug-in into Adobe's shared plug-in folder, so it works in every installed version of Premiere Pro and After Effects from 2019 on. It also checks which versions you have.

After installing, restart Premiere Pro and find it under Effects > Video Effects > PRO PLUGS > LookMatch.
