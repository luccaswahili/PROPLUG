LookMatch by PRO PLUGS

Look matching and film print emulation for Premiere Pro and After Effects.

Works with every version from CC 2015 to 2026. One plug-in covers all of them, so there is nothing to choose: it installs into Adobe's shared plug-in folder, which every Premiere Pro, After Effects and Media Encoder version reads.

Requires macOS 10.9 or later. Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Quit Premiere Pro and After Effects before installing.
